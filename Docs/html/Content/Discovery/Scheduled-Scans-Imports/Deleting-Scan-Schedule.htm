Deleting a Scan Schedule

  1. Tick the checkbox(es) for the job(s) to remove.

  2. Click Select Actions → Delete.

  1. In the Delete Confirmation dialog, type Delete exactly, then click Delete.

  2. No scans will be executed from the point of delete

  3. If an active scan is currently running, The scan will complete.

  4. Scan details or its history will not be accessible
