Email Notifications

Various functions in the application will generate an email notification when certain actions take place.

The user receives a notification based upon their email preferences as specified in their user account. (Admin > Users).

Example Functions

The list of functions that generate an email notification is too long to list in this topic. However, below are some examples:

When creating a request or creating an incident, an email notification is sent to Requester, Assignee, and to users based upon their email preferences.
When adding a new CI component, an email notification is sent to the applicable Roles as per the SACM RACI matrix and email preferences.
When creating or updating a project, an email notification is sent to Project Manager(s) associated with the project.
When an SLA breach occurs, a notification email is sent to Change Manager(s) and CAB members.
